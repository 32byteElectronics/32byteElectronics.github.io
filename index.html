WHAT WE DO

Engineering services for electronics products

From a circuit concept to a working prototype, Byte Electronics supports the practical stages of product development.

01

PCB Design

Schematic capture, PCB layout, component selection, footprints, design review, Gerber generation and prototype-ready documentation.

  • 2-layer & multi-layer layouts
  • Design for manufacturing
  • BOM & Gerber preparation
02

Manual PCB Assembly

Prototype and small-batch hand assembly for through-hole, SMD and mixed-technology boards.

  • THT & SMD assembly
  • Component replacement & rework
  • Prototype assembly
03

Custom Electronics

Custom controllers and electronic products designed around application requirements, available components and practical manufacturing constraints.

  • ESP32-based controllers
  • Relay controllers
  • Custom electronic interfaces
04

IoT Solutions

Connected hardware for monitoring and control using Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and cloud-connected architectures where required.

  • ESP32 development
  • Device monitoring & control
  • IoT prototypes
05

Android App Development

Android applications for IoT device monitoring and control, including user interfaces designed around the connected device.

  • IoT dashboards
  • Wi-Fi/Bluetooth control
  • Device-focused mobile UI
06

Prototype Development

Rapid proof-of-concept development combining electronics, firmware and application interfaces to validate an idea before production.

  • Proof of concept
  • Hardware integration
  • Functional testing
